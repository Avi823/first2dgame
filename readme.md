Welcome to Smooshed! This is a platformer (and my first game) using Unity! 

How to Play:
- Movement: Arrow Keys or WASD (with either w, up arrow, or the space bar to jump)
- T to revert to the last checkpoint
- R to revert smoosh state
- C to dash
- P to pause
What is it:
- You play as a ball (but a cool ball trust me) and are navigating through a dark, winter landscape to reach the end (the goal). However, you are graced with one power, and one power only: The Power to Be Smooshed 😭. Essentially, by getting hit into a wall by moving platforms, you can become either shorter or taller, letting you fit into gaps or inaccessible areas.  Probably best played in Fullscreen and on Computer (no mobile support).
